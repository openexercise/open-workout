# Code of conduct

Open Workout adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which applies in this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone represents the project.

In short: be welcoming and considerate, criticise ideas rather than people, and accept that maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that don't follow it.

## Reporting

Write to maintainers@openexercise.org. Reports are read by the maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), kept confidential, and answered within a week. If a report concerns a maintainer, that maintainer takes no part in handling it.

Maintainers follow the Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on the impact of what happened.
